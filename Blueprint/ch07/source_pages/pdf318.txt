7.9
Error Expansion for Symmetric Langevin Methods
Of particular interest for sampling the canonical distribution are symmetric
Langevin methods. We believe these are likely to be the most useful class of
methods for practitioners, as by symmetrizing the expansion the odd order terms
in (7.16) vanish identically using the Jacobi identity in the BCH expansions. This
implies that a symmetric scheme gives a second order error in computed averages.
Many symmetric methods can be constructed that require only one evaluation of the
force per iteration (effectively making them as inexpensive as a ﬁrst order method).

which will not alter the major results and amounts to a linear change of variables.
