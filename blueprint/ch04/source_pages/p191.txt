liquid with permanent dipoles [388] treats this as a system of rigid bodies (spheres)

of Chap. 1. From a computational and modelling perspective, there is no real reason
to use spheres (and perhaps good reasons not to); for example they might be taken
to be ellipsoidal. Let us assume that they are identical ellipsoids. The Hamiltonian
