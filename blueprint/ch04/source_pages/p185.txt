                                   OP D p   hg0.q/T   ;

               Q D q C hM  1 OP;

                  P D OP C hF.Q/   hg0.Q/T   ;

is deﬁned by ﬁrst order maps which reduce to the Symplectic Euler method and its
adjoint, this is an analogous scheme to the Störmer-Verlet method. Interestingly, this
method is not equivalent to SHAKE or RATTLE.
