8.4.3
Numerical Comparison of Dynamics
We shall compare the long-time averages evaluated by computing solution trajec-
tories for Nosé-Hoover, Langevin and Nosé-Hoover-Langevin dynamics. We will
look at the impact that the choice of dynamics (and associated parameters) has on
the rate of convergence of averages in time, as well as the perturbation introduced
into dynamical quantities (such as autocorrelation functions).
