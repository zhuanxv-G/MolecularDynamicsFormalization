We also brieﬂy compare DPD and PNHL methods. In Fig. 8.9 we show the
kinetic and conﬁgurational temperature errors as functions of the stepsize. The
results show the advantage of using the PNHL in terms of providing a more accurate
sampling for given stepsize; the result is especially important since the PNHL
methods are also more efﬁcient in practice. In Fig. 8.10 the velocity autocorrelation
functions are contrasted for different values of the friction coefﬁcient. This graph
