6.3  Stochastic Differential Equations


In this section we brieﬂy derive a more general stochastic differential equation as
the limit of a biased random walk. The treatment here is intuitive, based on the
concepts of limiting processes and stochastic integration introduced above. For a
rigorous treatment of the material presented here, it is recommended to consult a
reference such as the excellent book of Nelson [279] (which also contains a very
lively historical discussion).
