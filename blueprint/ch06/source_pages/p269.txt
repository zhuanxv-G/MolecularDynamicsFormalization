In the case of an SDE, the distribution may spread out over time. Even a single phase

diffuse as the solution of the Fokker-Planck equation and, eventually, would cover
the entire phase space. The incorporation of randomness has clearly changed the
picture of the evolving phase space distribution in a fundamental way.
