  An important point is that the formula of Rugh may not be robust from a
computational perspective in cases where the denominator may be close to zero.
The simple choice of the kinetic temperature is often desirable since it works for
both the inﬁnite domain and for periodic boundary conditions and is such that the
denominator is a nonzero constant.
   Rugh’s principle gives generalized temperature indicators on an inﬁnite domain,
but as we have already pointed out, typical molecular dynamics models are conﬁned
by periodic boundary conditions to a torus in conﬁguration space (or a toric-cylinder
in phase space). Similar results may be obtained in this case. The key point in the
proof above is the vanishing contribution from the boundary as R ! 1; in the
