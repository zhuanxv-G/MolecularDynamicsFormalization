### MolecularDynamics.Chapter06Review.scalarForward [MolecularDynamics/Chapter06/ReviewDefinitions.lean:129]
```lean
def scalarForward (a b ρ : ℝ → ℝ) (x : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  -deriv (fun z ↦ a z*ρ z) x + deriv (deriv (fun z ↦ b z^2*ρ z)) x/2
```
