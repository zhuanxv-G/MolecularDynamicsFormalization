3.5  Energy Conserving Methods


It is possible to derive numerical methods that exactly (i.e. to within rounding
error) conserve the energy at each step and this may seem a more natural approach
to molecular dynamics than symplectic integration since we have discussed and
evaluated methods in terms of their energy conservation.
   Consider the following 2D example of a differential equation system

                                            Pu D f.u; v/;      Pv D f.u; v/:

Notice that this system has a ﬁrst integral

                                   I.u; v/ D u   v;

and consider the application of Euler’s method:

             unC1 D un C hf.un; vn/;   vnC1 D vn C hf.un; vn/:
