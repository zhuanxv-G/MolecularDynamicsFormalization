In this section, we shall give an approach that allows the computation of the errors
introduced by the discretization of Langevin dynamics for more general potential
energy functions. The discretization schemes we shall consider will be those created
by composition of the A, B and O splitting pieces described earlier in this chapter,
but in principle we can apply this analysis to a wider class of SDE methods.

discretization error introduced by our SDE integration scheme (see [221–223]),
based on expansions due to Talay and Tubaro [357, 358].
