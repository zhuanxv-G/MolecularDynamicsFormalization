The self-diffusion coefﬁcient gives a metric quantifying the diffusion rate. It is
often used as a way to compare the rate of phase space exploration between methods,
and is typically calculated using the integral of the velocity auto-correlation
function. However, in theory one can construct arbitrary methods to artiﬁcially scale
the velocity auto-correlation function, hence giving inaccurate diffusion constants:
the momentum sampled may not be the momentum we use to propagate the position
with.
